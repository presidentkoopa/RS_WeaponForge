# Where the hands go — the owner's own calls

Decisions made by the owner looking at renders, 2026-09-30. These are not measurements and
must not be overwritten by one. Where a measurement disagrees with a line in this file, the
measurement is wrong.

The trigger hand is the `grip` `seat` on the card. The support hand is the off hand and is
a separate point, which on several of these is **on a moving part** and so has to travel
with it rather than sit still in gun space.

| Gun | Trigger hand | Support hand |
|---|---|---|
| BD_SSG | confirmed on the wrist of the sawn-off stock | under the barrel, at the front |
| BD_M79 | confirmed on the trigger group | under the fore-barrel |
| BD_MP40 | the angled handle behind the magazine housing | **the magazine itself** |
| BD_Minigun | the big handle at the rear of the weapon | a handle coming off the side |
| BD_Chainsaw | the rear handle, behind the motor | the bar over the top |
| BD_BFG | the handle hanging at the rear | under the barrel, forward |
| BD_Axe | one palm up from the pommel | 2.2 palms up the haft |
| BD_Dragonslayer | one palm up from the pommel | 2.2 palms up the haft |
| BD_Grenade | the object's own centre, one hand | none — it is thrown, not swung |

## The MP40 is the important one

The off hand holds **the magazine**. That is a moving part with a feed dof, so a support
point stored as a fixed point in gun space is wrong there by construction: drop the
magazine and the off hand is left holding the air where it used to be. It is the same
defect as a pump shotgun's forend, and it is why a support point has to name a PART and
ride it rather than name a place.

## The five exotics — his calls on the renders, 2026-09-30

He rejected the measured grip on all five. Every one of them is held like a rifle, which
is the pattern the measurement kept missing because it was ranking shapes rather than
asking what the gun IS.

| Gun | Trigger hand | Support hand |
|---|---|---|
| BD_Plasma | the grip low and to the rear — "it is a rifle" | under the forebarrel |
| BD_Railgun | rifle grip | under the forebarrel, just beyond the magwell |
| BD_RPG | the handle sticking DOWN, below the ammo cylinder | under the front of the launcher, where the rocket leaves |
| BD_Flamethrower | high and to the rear | what the first render highlighted at 16.07, -1.37, -5.43 |
| BD_Unmaker | low and to the rear | under the mouth, like a rifle |

THE FLAMETHROWER IS THE USEFUL ONE: the point the chain picked as its GRIP he identified
as the SUPPORT. That is the third time a pick has landed on the off hand's place rather
than the trigger hand's (the MP40 and an early shotgun candidate were the others), and it
is the clearest statement yet that shape alone cannot tell the two hands apart. Only the
trigger can.
